What is being built, and in what order

Live on the Pi, collecting from production eBay UK. Counted from the store, not quoted from the last edit: 5,617 listings, 26 completed sales, 179 coins judged by hand, 4 learned rules, 201 tests green. Browse spend 732 of 5,000 today; Trading 6.

One dependency gates a disproportionate share of everything below: completed sales. The tool gains about one a day, and every clearing figure rests on them. Six of the ten coin types now show no clearing price at all — correctly, because they have fewer than three sales each. That is not a bug to fix, it is a wait.

The second gate is Buy-It-Now outcomes, and it is structural. 94% of the live market is Buy-It-Now and not one of its outcomes has ever been observed, because a BIN listing carries no end time and the resolution queue only accepts listings whose end time has passed. COL-01 is the fix and it is deliberately parked — see the row.
